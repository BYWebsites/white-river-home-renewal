# White River Home Renewal

Build a modern, professional, high-converting website for a general contracting company called White River Contractors Inc.

COMPANY INFORMATION
- Company Name: White River Contractors Inc
- Industry: General Contracting
- Location: 1924 N Birch Ave, Fayetteville, AR 72703
- Phone: (479) 409-4695
- Ownership: Veteran-Owned and Latino-Owned Business
- Service Area: Northwest Arkansas and surrounding areas
- Services: Full-service general contracting. Residential and commercial projects. Remodeling, renovations, repairs, construction management, property improvements, and custom contracting solutions. The company handles projects of all sizes and welcomes all types of contracting requests.

WEBSITE GOAL
The primary goal is to generate leads and encourage visitors to call or submit a quote request. The design should immediately communicate trust, professionalism, quality workmanship, reliability, and customer service.

DESIGN STYLE
- Clean, modern, and premium appearance
- Professional construction industry aesthetic
- White, dark navy, and construction-inspired accent colors
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Large high-quality construction imagery
- Easy navigation
- Fast loading
- Strong calls-to-action throughout the website
